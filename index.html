Common questions

Everything you need to know

What kind of printers does it support?

It supports Bluetooth Classic, Bluetooth Low Energy, and Wi‑Fi TCP/IP thermal printers designed for 58mm receipt output.

Is this suitable for clinic or lab use?

Yes. It’s designed specifically for healthcare documentation, patient slips, and practical point-of-care workflows.

Does it require a cloud account?

No. The app is built around a local, offline-friendly workflow with no account dependency required.

Can I export records as PDF?

Yes. The app supports PDF export and Android native print workflows for flexible clinical documentation.
